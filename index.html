Privacy Policy for ZIM Provisional prep

Effective Date: October 6, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ZIM Provisional prep (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the App, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

A. Personal Data

The App is designed to minimize the collection of personal data. We do not require you to create an account with an email address or password. However, to provide our services and manage premium licenses, we collect:

  • Device ID: We collect a unique device identifier to generate and verify your premium activation code (either offline or via our remote servers).
  • Payment Verification: If you purchase a premium license via WhatsApp or EcoCash, we may collect your phone number and transaction reference number to process and activate your license.

B. Usage Data and Local Storage

  • App Activity: Your mock test scores, study plan progress, and flashcard mastery status are saved locally on your device to track your progress.
  • Device Time: We check your device's system time against internet time to validate license expiration and prevent tampering.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Activate and manage your premium subscription (Lifetime or Monthly passes).
  • Provide offline activation capabilities securely.
  • Prevent fraud, unauthorized distribution of licenses, and system time tampering.
  • Allow you to seamlessly track your exam readiness and study progress.
  • Schedule and send local study reminder notifications.

4. Disclosure of Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may disclose your information only in the following situations:

  • Service Providers: We may use third-party services (such as Cloudflare) for license verification and backend infrastructure.
  • Legal Obligation: If required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

5. Security of Your Information

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to help protect your information. Your device ID is hashed securely when generating offline activation codes to prevent reverse engineering. While we have taken reasonable steps to secure the personal information you provide to us, please be aware that no security measures are perfect or impenetrable.

6. Third-Party Services

The App contains links to external applications (such as WhatsApp) to facilitate customer support and premium upgrades. Once you leave the App, any information you provide is governed by the privacy policy of the third-party platform.

7. Children's Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.
